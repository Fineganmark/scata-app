Terms of use, safety and privacy

Version 0.1, November 2026

Draft for the pilot. This is a plain-language draft while Scata is being tested with a small number of families. It will be reviewed by a solicitor before Scata launches more widely, and you’ll be told when it changes.

1. What Scata is

Scata helps parents find other families making the same trip, to a school, a sports club or another group, and share those trips with a shared rota. Scata is a tool for arranging trips between parents. It is not a transport company, and Scata does not drive, supervise or carry any child.

2. Who can use it

  • You must be 18 or over and a parent or guardian of the children you add.
  • Use your real name. Families in your commute see your first and last name.
  • Schools and clubs that set up a group are responsible for who they invite to it.

3. Your safety responsibilities

Every trip is arranged between parents, who are responsible for it. When you share trips through Scata you agree that:

  • you will always travel with your own child for the whole journey;
  • if you drive, your car is roadworthy, taxed, has a valid NCT where needed, and is insured for the trip, and you hold a full driving licence;
  • every child in your car has the right child seat or booster for their age, height and weight, and wears a seatbelt;
  • if you walk or cycle with a group, you will supervise the children in it for the whole journey;
  • you won’t charge other families for a trip. Sharing trips is between neighbours, not a paid service, and payment could affect your insurance;
  • you will confirm or swap your rota slots in good time, and tell the others if you can’t do a trip.

Scata does not vet, check or screen parents, cars or licences. Decide for yourself who you are comfortable sharing trips with, and speak to the other parents before your first trip together.

4. What Scata is not responsible for

Because trips are arranged and made by parents, Scata is not responsible for anything that happens on a trip, for a trip that doesn’t happen, or for what other users do. Nothing in these terms takes away rights you have under Irish or EU consumer law.

5. Scata Plus

Scata Plus adds live tracking and chat. It is free during the pilot and no payment is taken. If it becomes a paid plan, you’ll be told the price first and can choose whether to pay.

6. Your data and privacy

Scata follows the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Here is what we keep and why:

  • Your account: your name and email address, to sign you in and to show other families in your commute who you are.
  • Your children: first names and dates of birth. Only you see these. Families in your commute see how many children you have.
  • Your home address or Eircode: only used to work out which families live near you. Other parents never see it, only a distance.
  • Your trips: how you travel, your times, your rota slots and chat messages, shared with the families in your commute.
  • Your location (Scata Plus only): only while you choose to share it during a trip, and only with the families in that commute. You can stop at any time.

Your data is stored in the EU (Ireland). Scata does not sell your data or show you advertising. You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time, and deleting your account removes it.

7. Ending your use

You can leave a commute or a group at any time from the app. Scata can remove an account that breaks these terms or puts children at risk.

8. Contact

Questions about these terms or your data: contact the Scata team through your school or club during the pilot. A contact email will be added here before launch.
